Firmware history

Saved across reboots

Keeps the latest 100 events per PCI address and hardware identity. Moving a GPU creates a separate history; replacing it with an identical card in the same slot cannot be distinguished. Historical readings and compatibility checks are not live status.

What does supported mean?

Detected means the Intel PCI device is visible. Inspectable means Intel's firmware interface responds. Compatible image means one newer GSC image passes the project, version and hardware checks.

These checks do not certify every board or generation. Integrated GPUs and devices without a firmware interface remain visible with an explanation. New generations need matching vendor firmware and support in the underlying Intel tool.

Preview releases do not promise recovery from a failed flash. A backup of the Unraid USB drive is not a GPU firmware backup.

Only the reviewed firmware catalog can supply flash images. Online release checks do not change that catalog.

CONFIRM GSC UPDATE

One GPU. One update.

The GPU must remain powered until the update finishes. Recovery or downgrade is not guaranteed. Other firmware components are not updated.
